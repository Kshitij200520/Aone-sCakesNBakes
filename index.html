Our Heritage Since 1952

Founded in 1952, Aone's Cakes N Bakes started with a simple promise: to bake with pure, uncompromised ingredients and traditional recipes handed down over generations.

What began as a neighborhood bakery famous for its melt-in-the-mouth 100% pure butter cookies and fresh morning pav buns has now grown into a full-fledged bakery & celebration hub featuring gourmet cakes, party decor, imported confectioneries, frozen delicacies, and festive gift hampers.
